- [What are seed registries actually accomplishing?](https://grain.org/en/article/7427-what-are-seed-registries-actually-accomplishing) (GRAIN with several organisations, 26 Oct 2026; received as the Spanish *Novedades de GRAIN* email, "¿Qué se está realmente logrando con los registros de semillas?", [Spanish version](https://grain.org/e/7429); the article page was read on 27 Oct): the right of rural communities to seeds faces a new global wave of regulation, especially the imposition of UPOV 91 and new marketing rules. Seed registries are put forward to protect peasant seed systems by recognising the quality of peasant seeds and the selection and breeding work behind them, but the publication argues they increasingly serve to extend intellectual property rules into local seed exchange: registration is becoming mandatory, the rules restrict even informal exchange and donation, and penalties can include prison. Its recommendations: recognition of peasant seed systems should not depend on registration, certification, DUS testing, quality-declared seed schemes or market integration; strengthen local, decentralised control over seeds; run public awareness campaigns; and use the UN Declaration on the Rights of Peasants (UNDROP) to hold governments accountable. Keywords: seed registries, peasant seed systems, UPOV 91, seed sovereignty, UNDROP, GRAIN. Related: [[Political_Ecology]].

- [Kuluri: find the hands, agree fair terms, keep the proof](https://lindangulopez.github.io/kuluri-site/) (Team 27, EU Agri-Hackathon 2026, Challenge 3 "Flowing data, empowered collaboration"; submitted 18 Oct 2026; code at [lindangulopez/kuluri-site](https://github.com/lindangulopez/kuluri-site), final 12-slide deck in its [`deck/`](https://github.com/lindangulopez/kuluri-site/tree/main/deck) folder): a prototype that connects farmers and seasonal workers, records the terms they agree before the work starts, and keeps a tamper-evident record of each agreement and each confirmed week of work. The pitch follows an organic olive grower and a seasonal worker in the Kastelli Valley, Crete, and ties the need to CAP social conditionality (Reg. 2021/2115), under which payments depend on fair labour while the proof is still on paper. The site tells the hiring story step by step with screenshots of the running app (Vue 3, Quasar, Capacitor). Agreement fingerprints (SHA-256) are meant to be anchored on the Polygon Amoy testnet, where the contracts are deployed; the app does not call them yet. The demo runs on the organisers' synthetic dataset, which records 9,517 hours of labour on the chosen farm and no seasonal worker. Matching never uses nationality, age or gender. Keywords: seasonal work, farm labour, CAP social conditionality, labour agreements, verifiable records, hackathon. Related: [[Safe_and_Just]], [[Data]], [[Professional_Background]].

- [Study on the working conditions of farm employees in the EU agriculture sector](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/4541b8bd-4bee-11f0-85ba-01aa75ed71a1/language-en) (European Commission, DG AGRI; written by Milieu Law & Policy Consulting, final report April 2025, published June 2025, 189 pages; PDF filed privately, shared in a hackathon team chat): a baseline for evaluating **CAP social conditionality**, which since 1 January 2025 at the latest links CAP payments to compliance with EU labour and occupational-safety rules, with payment cuts from 1% to 100% depending on severity, extent, recurrence and intent. The study maps farm workers' conditions across the EU, the enforcement systems, and how CAP paying agencies and labour inspectorates share information. Figures it reports: around 60% of farm employment is informal (ILO); 32% of the EU's agricultural labour force lacks a written contract (European Platform on Undeclared Work); in Hungarian inspections of 2023, the most frequent violation (40% of cases) was a missing work schedule or falsified working hours. Its main finding on implementation: most paying agencies have no established channel with the enforcement bodies, and interoperable information systems are lacking, especially in decentralised countries such as Austria, Italy and Spain. Used as the evidence base for Linda's EU Agri-Hackathon project. Keywords: CAP, social conditionality, farm workers, seasonal work, undeclared work, paying agencies. Related: [[Safe_and_Just]], [[Data]].

- *From our soil to the UN General Assembly: Africa's women farmers demand change* (Greenpeace Africa, written by Elizabeth Atieno, Food Campaigner; shared via LinkedIn, `raw/Gmail - From our soil to the UN General Assembly_ Africa's women farmers demand change.pdf`, 19 Sept 2026): African women farmers are growing food under mounting climate pressure — unpredictable rainfall, intensifying droughts and floods, degrading agricultural land, rising input costs, and expanding corporate influence over seeds and food systems — and are responding by restoring soils, conserving crop diversity, managing water, and preserving agricultural knowledge that strengthens climate resilience. The UN designated 2026 the International Year of the Woman Farmer, meant to move discussion beyond recognition toward the policies, resources, and land/seed/finance/climate-investment rights women farmers have consistently demanded, given their continued underrepresentation in decisions that shape their productivity, income, and ability to recover from failed harvests. Greenpeace Africa Executive Director Dr. Oulie Keita is carrying this message to the UN General Assembly, arguing that women farmers' lived experience should inform government approaches to food security, climate adaptation, and agricultural development. Keywords: women farmers, Africa, UN General Assembly, International Year of the Woman Farmer, food sovereignty, climate resilience, seed rights. Related: [[Political_Ecology]], [[Safe_and_Just]], [[Climate_Change]].

- [Agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): Python package for delineating field boundaries. Combines seven AI approaches — object detection, semantic segmentation, vision transformers, and embedding-based clustering — to map agricultural field boundaries from Sentinel-2, Landsat, NAIP, and other satellite sources, while automatically filtering out non-agricultural areas using land cover data. Keywords: field boundary delineation, satellite imagery, deep learning, Sentinel-2, land cover. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Python]], [[Machine_Learning]], [[Embeddings]].
