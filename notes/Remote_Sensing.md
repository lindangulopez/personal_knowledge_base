- [The Technical Debt of Earth Embedding Products](https://cloudnativegeo.org/blog/2026/02/the-technical-debt-of-earth-embedding-products/): Article comparing different earth embeddings. Examines the fragmentation problem across nine major Earth embedding products (Clay, Major TOM, Earth Index, AlphaEarth, Tessera, Presto, and others) — each trains well on petabytes of satellite imagery but uses different file formats, tile schemes, and spatial references, creating technical debt and downstream costs for users trying to compare or combine embeddings. Keywords: earth embeddings, foundation models, satellite imagery, interoperability, technical debt. Related: [[Embeddings]], [[Machine_Learning]].
