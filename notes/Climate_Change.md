- *Renewable Energy Siting and the Côa Valley: Rewilding Portugal's SOPHIA Position* (`field-trips/deskStudy/renewable-energy-policy-coa-valley.md`): Rewilding Portugal's November 2025 opposition to the SOPHIA solar project (Fundão-Penamacor, 3,500 ha footprint, cutting the "Raia Norte"/"Raia Sul" ecological corridors) is not a blanket anti-solar position — it explicitly calls for siting on already-artificialised land (rooftops, brownfield) instead, backed by an environmental-justice argument (rural Beira Interior hosting transition costs without proportional benefit). Asks whether that project-specific statement should generalize into a standing policy position, and surveys three precedents: the Netherlands' 2020 "zonneladder" (solar ladder, rooftops-first — well-articulated but under-enforced), Germany's mature EEG spatial-control regime since 2004, and Portugal's own live RED III transposition (the "Mapa Verde" go-to-areas consultation, June 2026) as the concrete process this question could plug into now. See [[Urban_Planning]] for the companion solar-siting/tourism-lodge infrastructure proposal this note leads into. Keywords: RED III, solar siting, ecological corridors, environmental justice, Mapa Verde, SOPHIA. Related: [[Safe_and_Just]], [[Urban_Planning]].

- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054): Open global dataset containing 2.6 million historical flood events derived from news articles spanning more than 150 countries, published February 2026 by a team of 15 researchers. Keywords: flood events, disaster data, news mining, global dataset, climate risk. Related: [[Data]], [[Remote_Sensing]].
