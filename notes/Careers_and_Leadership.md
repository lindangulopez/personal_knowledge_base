# Careers and Leadership

**Summary**: Career paths and leadership stories, especially of African women, read for how people grow into larger roles across countries and sectors.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-12 (Mapula Bodibe, MTN, from marketing to CEO)

---

- *Climbing the Corporate Ladder: How Mapula Bodibe Broke Barriers, Crossed Borders and Rose to CEO* (Valentine Zoza, Women Power Africa newsletter on LinkedIn, email of 11 Nov 2026; the email gives the opening only, link not followed): a profile of Mapula Bodibe, a South African executive and Chartered Marketer, over two decades in marketing and commercial roles at the MTN Group: Chief Marketing Officer of MTN Uganda, Chief Consumer Officer of MTN South Africa, CEO of MTN Rwanda and, as of 2026, CEO of MTN South Sudan. Her degrees are all from the University of South Africa (UNISA): a BCom in Economics, a Postgraduate Diploma in Marketing and an MBA. The article frames her career as continuous learning and leading across markets and cultures. Keywords: Mapula Bodibe, MTN, women in leadership, South Africa, telecommunications, UNISA.
