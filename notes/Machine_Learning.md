- *International Workshop on Artificial Intelligence for Scientific Platforms: AI-driven Innovation in Safety-Critical Applications* (ASNR information sheet, 2 pages, received 26 Oct 2026; no public web page found on 27 Oct, and the sheet's "workshop webpage" and "Register here" links did not survive in the PDF): a one-day, in-person seminar in English on **Tuesday 16 March 2027** at the Maison Irène et Frédéric Joliot-Curie, 100 rue du Trône, Brussels, organised by the French Authority for Nuclear Safety and Radiation Protection (ASNR) with CEA and CNRS. Subtitle: "The Essential Role of Experiments, Data and Expert Interpretation". Its key messages: rare, quality-assured data are a strategic scientific resource, and their relevance, traceability and context matter as much as their volume; data become valuable when combined with expertise; experimental platforms, which generate data under controlled conditions and can reproduce rare events, are key to trustworthy AI; computing, storage, secure data access, interoperable tools and skilled staff must be developed together; pooling data, facilities, experts and European programmes avoids duplication. The day mixes short scientific case studies (nuclear and non-nuclear, across physical, health and environmental sciences and engineering), institutional contributions and a round table, and also covers access to and governance of scientific data, computing, storage and data-transfer infrastructures, and opportunities for international organisations. Framed by the European Strategy for AI in Science. Audience: EU institutions and the JRC, Member State research attachés, national contact points, research and technical safety organisations, universities, research infrastructures, AI and data specialists, and nuclear experts (SNETP, ETSON, IAEA, NEA). Scientific committee tracks: data and AI infrastructures; health and life sciences; environment and monitoring; nuclear safety; emergency preparedness and crisis management. Keywords: AI in science, trustworthy AI, experimental data, data governance, research infrastructures, ASNR. Related: [[Data]], [[Reproducible_Science]].
